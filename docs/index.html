THE BONOBO CODE

A research project on female social networks, coalition, social infrastructure, autonomy, and collective power.

Central question

Did the weakening or fragmentation of women's independent social networks contribute to women's dependence on male-centered institutions, and can stronger female social networks increase women's collective autonomy and power?
Research standard. Every claim is labeled by evidence type, traced to a source, and tested against contradictory evidence. Bonobos are a comparative case, not a blueprint.
