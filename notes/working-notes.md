## Contemporary observation (research prompt, not evidence)

**TikTok LIVE women-centered space (author's observation).** A recurring livestream describes itself as women-centered. Reported norms: no arguing/debating; communicate rather than fight; facts over feelings; women not expected to coddle men or provide free emotional labor; men's feelings are not the organizing principle. The host is reported to be asexual and to say she does not desire a man. Men reportedly enter and challenge her specifically on her lack of interest in male romantic/sexual desire.

How this is treated:

- 🟡 It is a **single observation by one participant-observer**. It cannot support any general claim.
- It generates the questions listed in `research/08-digital-female-communities.md`.
- Ethics: do not record identifying details of the host or participants in this repo. Describe norms and dynamics, not people.
- Bias check: the observer is a regular, sympathetic viewer. Selection and confirmation bias are likely; note what would count as disconfirming (e.g., the space producing no friendship/aid, or reproducing exclusion).

## Terms to keep straight

| Term | Working definition |
|---|---|
| Female social infrastructure | Physical, social, cultural, and digital conditions that allow women to repeatedly encounter, know, support, cooperate with, and organize alongside other women |
| Female social ecosystem | The wider set of female relationships and settings a woman can draw on (broader than friendship) |
| Coalition | Repeated coordinated action by multiple individuals against or for a third party/interest |
| Collective power | Ability of a group to act together and secure outcomes (see `research/06`) |
| Network density | Share of possible ties among a set of people that actually exist |
